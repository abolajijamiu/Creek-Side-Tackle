CREEKSIDE TACKLE — TYPOGRAPHY

PRIMARY — Barlow Condensed (SemiBold 600, Bold 700)
  Headlines, the wordmark, navigation, product titles and packaging. Set in uppercase for display with slight tracking.
  https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Barlow+Condensed

SECONDARY — Barlow (Regular 400, Medium 500, SemiBold 600)
  Body copy, descriptions, buttons and interface text.
  https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Barlow

ACCENT — IBM Plex Mono (Medium 500)
  Specs only: line weight, hook size, lure weight, SKUs and dates.
  https://fonts.google.com/specimen/IBM+Plex+Mono
